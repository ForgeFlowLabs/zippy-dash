# Game Covers

3 cover images (landscape, portrait, square) + preview video are required at submission. Covers should be updated occasionally post-launch too.

Covers are the storefront — they need to grab attention in a fraction of a second among hundreds of other games in a category. A poor cover directly hurts performance (players scroll past).

## General guidance
- Look at successful games in the same genre for inspiration (Steam, Epic Games, etc. also good references).
- Update covers occasionally, especially alongside content updates or for seasonal events — but not too often, or players lose recognition.
- Hire a designer (Fiverr/Upwork) if needed.

## Cover Image Specs
- **Landscape** — 16:9, 1920x1080
- **Portrait** — 2:3, 800x1200
- **Square** — 1:1, 800x800
- All three must share consistent visuals/aesthetics so the game is recognizable across formats.

## Restrictions
- No borders around covers.
- No text other than the game's title (no "New", "Updated", "Play", "Play now", etc.)
- No icons or store logos.
- No copyrighted visuals you don't have rights to.
- No blurry/pixelated visuals.

## Guidelines
- **Don't just screenshot the game** — players can already preview gameplay by hovering. Use the main character as the visual focus, a stylized title, or an evocative (non-literal) graphic instead.
- **Keep it simple** — over-cluttered covers are hard to scan, especially on small screens.
- **Put the game's name directly on the cover** — makes it memorable and distinct from similar-style games.
- **Use a nice, stylized font** matching the game's aesthetic — avoid generic defaults like Times New Roman.

## Preview Video
Shown on hover over the thumbnail. Should capture the most exciting/visually appealing moments:
- Driving games: varied cars/maps/weather, dashboard-cam angles for immersion.
- Multiplayer: full lobby, multiple maps, biggest celebration/prize moment.
- Action games: intense multi-mode battles, varied weapons/killstreaks, notable builds.
- Progression-heavy games: show evolution/customization across different modes.
- Animated video is fine as long as it's representative of actual gameplay (not misleading).

**Video specs:**
- Length: 15-20 seconds max (longer gets cut to 20s automatically)
- Size: 50MB max
- Resolutions required: landscape 1080p (16:9) AND portrait 1080p (2:3)
- **Avoid:** black screen/logo transitions, black bars, default mouse cursor, "Play Now"/promo text, app/social icons
- No fast-forwarding (they auto-speed it up slightly during processing anyway)
- No sound
- Use the static cover image as the opening frame — creates a seamless thumbnail→video transition on hover

**Recommended tools:** ClipChamp (Windows) / iMovie (Mac) for editing; HandBrake for conversion.
